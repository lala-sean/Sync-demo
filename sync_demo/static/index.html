What is being fitted?

The constrained model optimizes one fixed 6-DoF camera extrinsic and one shared jaw length. Centerline and opening axes are selected in the UI. Tool-origin offset is zero by default; it can instead be supplied manually or explicitly added to the optimization.

Exploratory visual registration — not a validated hand-eye calibration or a transform for robot control. A good image fit does not establish physical accuracy.

Fit inspection

Residuals and geometry will appear here. No per-frame tracking or image snapping is performed.
